Community guidelines

Fellowship is for encouraging each other. Keep it that way. You may not post or send:

  • Bullying, harassment, threats, or attacks on anyone's faith, race, ethnicity, nationality, gender, sexual orientation, disability or age
  • Sexual content, nudity, or anything that sexualizes minors
  • Violence, encouragement of self-harm, or content promoting illegal activity
  • Spam, scams, impersonation, or other people's private information
  • Content you don't have the right to share

Disagreeing about faith is fine. Being cruel about it isn't.

Reporting and blocking

Every post, comment, friend and AI-generated guide or journey has a Report option, and you can block anyone. Blocked people disappear from your feed, friends, groups and duels. Wolf & Crow reviews reports and may remove content or accounts that break these guidelines, without notice.

AI features

Some talking points, quizzes and journeys are drafted by AI. AI can be wrong. They are study aids, not Scripture and not your pastor. Always check them against the Bible and your church. Please report anything offensive or inaccurate with the Report button.
