Timeworn — Privacy Policy

Effective: November 19, 2026

Timeworn ("the app") is published by Wolf & Crow LLC of Tennessee ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app collects, why, and who it is shared with.

Your account

To list items or post, you create an account with your email, a password and a display name (Google Firebase Authentication). Your email is never shown to other members. You can delete your account and your posts any time in Settings, or ask us to delete it even without the app.

What you post is public

Listings (photos, descriptions, price, town and any contact info you add), Parlor threads, replies, questions and shop notes are stored with Google Firebase and visible to everyone using the app, with your display name.

Reports and blocking

When you report something, the reason, a short excerpt and the item's ID are stored in Firebase for review. Members you block, and posts you hide, are remembered on your phone only.

Location

If you allow location or type a ZIP code, it is used to find nearby shops: it is sent to Google (Maps and Places) and, as a backup, Geoapify. We don't store your location.

Other services

Shop details come from Google Places and OpenStreetMap, under their own terms and privacy policies.

Permissions

Location (optional, for "near me"), and photos you choose to attach.

What we don't do

We don't sell your information, show ads, or use advertising or analytics trackers.

Children

You must be 18 or older to use the Market. The app is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
