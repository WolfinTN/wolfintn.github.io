Delete your Sharpen account

In the app: open Sharpen → ⚙️ Settings → Account → Delete my account. It happens immediately.

Without the app: email wolf.crow.tn.llc@gmail.com from the email you signed in with, subject "Delete my Sharpen account." We'll delete it within 30 days and reply when it's done.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (Google or email sign-in)
  • Your progress backup
  • Your profile, friend code, friendships and group memberships
  • Your posts, and your push notification registration

Comments you left on other people's posts may remain until they're deleted by you or the post's owner, or within 30 days of an emailed request. Reports you filed are kept up to 90 days for safety review. Progress stored only on your phone stays until you uninstall the app.
