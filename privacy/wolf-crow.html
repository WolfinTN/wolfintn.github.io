Wolf & Crow — Privacy Policy

Effective: November 19, 2026

Wolf & Crow ("the app") is published by Wolf & Crow LLC. The app is a directory of other Wolf & Crow apps.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts, collects no personal information, and contains no ads, analytics or tracking.

How the app works

  • App list: The app downloads a public list of Wolf & Crow apps over the internet. This request carries no personal information.
  • Installed apps: The app checks, on your device only, whether specific Wolf & Crow apps are installed, so it can show "Open" or "Update." This information never leaves your device.
  • Links: Buttons open Google Play or web pages. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
