Daily Bread — Privacy Policy

Effective: November 19, 2026

Daily Bread ("the app") is published by Wolf & Crow LLC of Tennessee ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app collects, why, and who it is shared with.

Stored on your phone only

Your topics, saved and learned lessons, reflection notes, theme and reminder settings stay on your phone. We can't see them. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Sent over the internet

  • AI lessons: to write new lessons, the app sends the topic names you picked or typed to our server (a Cloudflare Worker), which passes them to OpenRouter and the AI model it routes to. No name, email or account is sent. Your reflection notes are never sent. Some free AI providers may keep requests to improve their models, so don't put private details in custom topic names. If you enter your own OpenRouter key, requests go straight to OpenRouter under your account.
  • Reports: if you tap Report on a lesson, the lesson text and the reason you chose are sent to our server and kept in its logs for a few days so we can review them.
  • Bible verses are fetched from bible-api.com. "Read" and "Go deeper" links open Bible Gateway, Wikipedia, the Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy or YouTube in your browser, under their own privacy policies.

Permissions

Notifications, for the optional daily reminder.

What we don't do

We don't sell your information, show ads, or use advertising or analytics trackers.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has shared information in the app, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
