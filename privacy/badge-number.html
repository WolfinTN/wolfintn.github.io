What's Your Badge Number? — Privacy Policy

Effective: November 19, 2026

What's Your Badge Number? ("the app") is published by Wolf & Crow LLC of Tennessee ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app collects, why, and who it is shared with.

No accounts

The app signs you in anonymously in the background (Google Firebase), so you never give a name or email. Your phone gets a random ID that is attached to what you post, so you can delete it later.

What you post is public

  • Stories and replies: the text you write and the details you choose (service, years on the job, optional sign-off name, category) are stored with Google Firebase and shown to everyone who uses the app.
  • "Feeling Brave?" recordings: audio or video you attach is uploaded to Cloudinary and is public, including your voice and, for video, your face.
  • Reports: when you report a story or reply, the reason and the post are stored in Firebase so we can review it.

Please follow the community rules: no real names, addresses, plate or case numbers, and nothing from an open case. You can delete your own stories and replies in the app. To ask us to remove something, email us.

Other services

  • Scanner audio comes from OpenMHz. If you look up a ZIP code, it is sent to Zippopotam.us to find your area.
  • YouTube videos play through YouTube's own player, so YouTube (Google) receives your device's request to play them. See the Google Privacy Policy and YouTube Terms of Service.

Stored on your phone

Posts and people you hid, and your settings. ("Respect" taps are saved with the story.)

Permissions

Microphone, only when you record a "Feeling Brave?" story. Video is recorded with your phone's camera app.

What we don't do

We don't sell your information, show ads, or use advertising or analytics trackers.

Children

The app is meant for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has shared information in the app, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
