Last Stand — Privacy Policy

Effective: November 19, 2026

Last Stand ("the app") is published by Wolf & Crow LLC of Tennessee ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app collects, why, and who it is shared with.

Information we collect

None. There are no accounts. Your favorites, the clips you add and your settings stay on your phone.

Sent over the internet

  • YouTube videos play through YouTube's own player, so YouTube (Google) receives your device's request to play them. See the Google Privacy Policy and YouTube Terms of Service.
  • The app may download an updated list of scenes from GitHub. This request carries no personal information.

What we don't do

We don't sell your information, show ads, or use advertising or analytics trackers.

Children

The app is for adults 18 and older and is not directed to children. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
