Sharpen Privacy Policy

Wolf & Crow LLC · Last updated November 27, 2026

Sharpen works without an account. Signing in is optional, and Wolf & Crow LLC never sells personal information or uses it for ads.

What stays on your phone

Your XP, streak, journey progress, collected cards, quiz scores, church code or channel, sermon notes, sermon transcripts, chosen translation and any leader drafts are saved only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Microphone

Sharpen only uses the microphone when you tap "Listen" to have it write down a sermon. On phones that support it, speech is turned into text entirely on the phone. On other phones, Android's speech service (provided by Google) does the transcription. Audio is never saved by Sharpen.

AI features

Talking points, quizzes and custom journeys are made by the AI built into your phone when it has one. Otherwise the sermon text (transcript or notes) or your journey topic is sent to OpenRouter, through a Wolf & Crow server on Cloudflare, to generate them. Nothing is stored on that server.

Services the app connects to

  • bible-api.com to load Scripture text. The app sends only the passage requested.
  • wolfintn.github.io (GitHub Pages) to load your church's weekly file. The app sends only the church code.
  • YouTube to play videos (YouTube's embedded player) and list your church channel's newest uploads. Video and channel searches go through the YouTube Data API on Wolf & Crow's server. Sharpen uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) to deliver notifications from your friends.
  • wolfintn.github.io also provides new journeys. The app only downloads them.

These services may log standard technical information such as your IP address, as any website does.

Friends & crews (optional)

If you sign in (with Google or email, through Google Firebase Authentication), Sharpen stores your email address, a backup of your app progress, and, if you join Fellowship, the first name you choose, a friend code, a summary of your progress (rank, XP, streak, cards, current journey), your posts, reactions, comments and duel scores, and a push notification token, all in Google Firebase. Only friends you add and crews you join can see them; there is no public feed. You can turn off progress sharing and notifications, block or report people, and leave the community in Settings, or delete your whole account there or at this page. Reports are reviewed by Wolf & Crow LLC.

Sharing

When a leader uses "Share this week's file," the app hands the text to the sharing app you choose. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

Children

Sharpen does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
