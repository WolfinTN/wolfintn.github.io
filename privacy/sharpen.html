Sharpen Privacy Policy

Wolf & Crow LLC · Last updated November 27, 2026

Sharpen does not require an account and never sells personal information.

What stays on your phone

Your XP, streak, journey progress, collected cards, quiz scores, church code or channel, sermon notes, sermon transcripts, chosen translation and any leader drafts are saved only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Microphone

Sharpen only uses the microphone when you tap "Listen" to have it write down a sermon. On phones that support it, speech is turned into text entirely on the phone. On other phones, Android's speech service (provided by Google) does the transcription. Audio is never saved by Sharpen.

AI features

Talking points, quizzes and custom journeys are made by the AI built into your phone when it has one. Otherwise the sermon text (captions, transcript or notes) or your journey topic is sent to OpenRouter, through a Wolf & Crow server on Cloudflare, to generate them. Nothing is stored on that server.

Services the app connects to

  • bible-api.com to load Scripture text. The app sends only the passage requested.
  • wolfintn.github.io (GitHub Pages) to load your church's weekly file. The app sends only the church code.
  • YouTube to play videos, list your church channel's newest uploads and read sermon captions. YouTube's own privacy policy applies.
  • wolfintn.github.io also provides new journeys. The app only downloads them.

These services may log standard technical information such as your IP address, as any website does.

Friends & crews (optional)

If you join the community, Sharpen stores the first name you choose, a friend code, and a summary of your progress (rank, XP, streak, cards, current journey) in Google Firebase, along with posts, reactions and comments you make. Only friends you add and crews you join can see them; there is no public feed. Sign-in is anonymous: no email or password. You can turn off progress sharing, block or report people, and leave the community in Settings, which deletes your profile, posts and friendships. Reports are reviewed by Wolf & Crow LLC.

Sharing

When a leader uses "Share this week's file," the app hands the text to the sharing app you choose. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

Children

Sharpen does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
