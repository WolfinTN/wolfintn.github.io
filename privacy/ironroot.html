Ironroot — Privacy Policy

Effective: November 23, 2026

Ironroot ("the app") is published by Wolf & Crow LLC of Tennessee ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app collects, why, and who it is shared with.

Stored on your phone only

Your sobriety date, streak, check-ins and moods, your "why", journal entries, voice and video recordings, photos, trusted contacts, the last place you searched for meetings, and settings are stored on your phone. We can't see them, and there is no account. Uninstalling the app deletes them. Contacts you add are picked one at a time with Android's contact picker; the app never reads your address book.

Sent over the internet

  • AI chat: when you use the chat, your messages, plus a short summary so replies fit you (days sober, recent moods, your "why", and what you're staying sober from), are sent through our server (a Cloudflare Worker) to OpenRouter and the AI model it routes to. Our server doesn't store them. Some free AI providers may keep requests to improve their models, so avoid sharing names or other identifying details in the chat. Chat history on your phone is cleared when the app is fully closed. If you enter your own OpenRouter key, messages go straight to OpenRouter under your account.
  • Reports: if you tap Report on an AI reply, that reply and the reason you chose are sent to our server and kept in its logs for a few days so we can review them. Your own messages are not sent.
  • Find a meeting: to list Narcotics Anonymous meetings, the app looks up the ZIP code or city you type, or your approximate location if you tap "Use my location", and sends those coordinates, rounded to about 1 km, to NA's public meeting list service (the BMLT aggregator) to get nearby meetings. Map searches open your map app, and other meeting finders open in your browser; those services' own privacy policies apply there. We don't receive or keep your location.
  • YouTube videos play through YouTube's own player, so YouTube (Google) receives your device's request to play them. See the Google Privacy Policy and YouTube Terms of Service.

Health information

Sobriety and mood information is sensitive. We designed the app so it stays on your phone, except what you choose to send in the AI chat as described above. The app is not a medical service. If you're in crisis, call or text 988.

Permissions

Microphone (voice and video journal, only when you record), approximate location (only when you tap "Use my location" in Find a meeting), notifications and exact alarms (reminders and milestones).

What we don't do

We don't sell your information, show ads, or use advertising or analytics trackers.

Children

The app is meant for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has shared information in the app, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
