Railside — Privacy Policy

Effective: November 19, 2026

Railside ("the app") is published by Wolf & Crow LLC of Tennessee ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app collects, why, and who it is shared with.

No accounts

The app signs you in anonymously in the background (Google Firebase), so you never give a name or email. Your phone gets a random ID that is attached to anything you share.

What you share is public

Train sightings you choose to share (railroad, engine number, direction, town, note and the handle you set) and anything you post are stored with Google Firebase and shown to other Railside users. Towns only: never share a home address or an exact trackside spot. Sightings you don't share stay on your phone. When you report something, the reason is stored in Firebase for review.

Other services

  • Scanner audio comes from OpenMHz. A town or ZIP you enter is sent to Zippopotam.us, OpenStreetMap (Overpass) and Wikidata to find nearby railroads and places.
  • Amtrak train positions come from Amtraker. Night train sounds are downloaded once from Wikimedia Commons.
  • Weekly Rail School content is downloaded from our Firebase project.
  • YouTube videos play through YouTube's own player, so YouTube (Google) receives your device's request to play them. See the Google Privacy Policy and YouTube Terms of Service.

Permissions

Notifications and background audio, so the scanner and night sounds keep playing with the screen off.

What we don't do

We don't sell your information, show ads, or use advertising or analytics trackers.

Children

The app is meant for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has shared information in the app, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
