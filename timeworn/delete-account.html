Timeworn — Delete your account

Timeworn is an app by Wolf & Crow LLC. You can delete your account and its data in the app, or ask us to do it here, even if you no longer have the app.

Ask us to delete it

Email us from the address you signed up with, or tell us that address, and we'll delete your account and its data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

Request account deletion

Or email wolf.crow.tn.llc@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Timeworn account".

Or delete it in the app

  1. Open Timeworn and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings and tap Delete my account.
  3. Enter your password to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: email, password and display name.
  • Your listings and Parlor threads, with their photos.
  • Deleting in the app keeps the notes and replies you left on other members' posts, under your name. If you ask us by email, we delete those too.

Reports you filed about other members' posts may be kept until we finish reviewing them. Nothing else is kept.
